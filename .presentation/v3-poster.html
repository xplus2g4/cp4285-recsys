Fixing one bias, shifting another

What single-bias debiasing does to the biases it doesn’t target

A · B · C · D · E · F
National University of Singapore

Recommenders learn from what users were shown, not from what they would have liked. Debiasing methods fix this one bias at a time and are judged on accuracy alone [2]. We measured every bias on randomly exposed videos [1] and asked: when a method removes its bias, where does the bias go?

Takeaways

    Limits. One dataset, one backbone. The tuned learning rate changes for some knob values. Lower ARP is not always better. Per-user JSD has a floor: users click only a few random videos each. No multiple-comparison correction.
