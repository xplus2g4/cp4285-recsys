Methodology

MethodHow it corrects: training loss, ranking score
MACR [3]popularityAdds item branch \hat y_i and user branch \hat y_u; ranking removes the item effect
\mathcal L=\mathrm{BCE}\big(f_{ui}\,\sigma(\hat y_i)\,\sigma(\hat y_u),\,y_{ui}\big)+\alpha\,\mathrm{BCE}(\hat y_i,y_{ui})+\beta\,\mathrm{BCE}(\hat y_u,y_{ui})
s_{ui}=(f_{ui}-c)\,\sigma(\hat y_i)\,\sigma(\hat y_u)
IPS-feat [4]exposureWeights each logged interaction by 1/\hat P_{ui}; \hat P_{ui} = P(shown), from an FM over user and video features
\mathcal L=\textstyle\sum w_{ui}\,\mathrm{BCE}(f_{ui},y_{ui}),\ \ w_{ui}\propto\min\big(1/\hat P_{ui},\,\mathrm{clip}/\max\hat P\big),\quad s_{ui}=f_{ui}
D2Q [5]durationTrains on the watch-time quantile \hat\Phi_{k(i)} within the video’s length group (one of M); ranks by the watch time it implies
\mathcal L=\textstyle\sum\mathrm{BCE}\big(\sigma(f_{ui}),\,\hat\Phi_{k(i)}(w_{ui})\big),\quad s_{ui}=\hat\Phi^{-1}_{k(i)}\big(\sigma(f_{ui})\big)
